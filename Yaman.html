Plot

Yaman (English: Lord of Death) is a 2017 Indian Tamil political thriller film written and directed by Jeeva Shankar. Co-produced by Lyca Productions and Vijay Antony Film Corporation, the film features Vijay Antony and Miya George in the lead roles, while Thiagarajan portrays another pivotal character.
