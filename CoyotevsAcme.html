    Plot

    After all of his delivered ACME products consistently fail him while attempting to capture the Road Runner, Wile E. Coyote decides that enough is enough. He enlists Kevin Avery, an unlucky small-time lawyer working out of a budget billboard practice, to file a historic product liability lawsuit against the ACME Corporation. As word spreads of the groundbreaking cartoon-vs-corporation lawsuit, ACME sends their intimidating, high-powered corporate lawyer Buddy Crane—Kevin's arrogant former boss—to crush the case. Along with Kevin's quick-thinking paralegal niece Paige, the mismatched duo navigates hilarious courtroom drama, cartoon physical comedy, and deep corporate conspiracies to prove that Wile E. Coyote was never at fault. Source:IMDB
