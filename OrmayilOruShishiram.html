    Plot

    Ormayil Oru Shishiram is a poetic portrait of first love and its aftermath. The film narrates the love story of two youngsters named Nithin and Varsha, and their shared destiny. Directed by Vivek Aryan, the film stars Deepak Parambol and Anaswara Ponnambath in lead roles. Source:BookMyShow
