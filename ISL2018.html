Description

The 2018–19 Indian Super League season is the fifth season of the Indian Super League, one of the top Indian professional football leagues, since its establishment in 2013. The regular season began on 29 September 2018 and is scheduled to conclude in March 2019. The league will take a mid-season break for around one month from 17 December 2018 to early January 2019 due to India's participation in the 2019 AFC Asian Cup. SourceWikipedia
