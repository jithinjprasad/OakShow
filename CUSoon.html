    Plot

    Jimmy (Roshan Mathew) is working in a middle east company and he is in a relationship with Anu (Darshana Rajendran), whom he met via a dating app. Despite not meeting in person, Jimmy introduces Anu to his mother. Things become complicated as Anu sends a suicide note-video to Jimmy and Police takes Jimmy into custody. To prove Jimmy's innocence and find the mystery, Jimmy's family seeks help of his cousin - Kevin (Fahadh Faasil).Source:Wikipedia
