Premise

"Morgan" is not like other girls. Created and raised in a laboratory environment by a group of scientists, she defies nature with her super-human qualities. Walking and talking within one month and self-sufficient after just six, her rapid progression is remarkable, exceeding the expectations of her creators. After Morgan has a "tantrum" in which she viciously attacks and injures one of the scientists , corporate troubleshooter Lee Weathers is called in to determine whether or not to terminate Morgan before she can cause any more havoc or escape to the outside world.
