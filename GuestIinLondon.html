Plot

The film is a comedy revolving around Kartik Aaryan's character, who is in a live-in relationship with his girlfriend (Kriti Kharbanda) when Paresh Rawal and his wife (Tanvi Azmi) barge into his home as guests, setting off a laugh-riot. Source: Wikipedia
