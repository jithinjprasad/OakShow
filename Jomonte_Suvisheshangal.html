Plot

Dulquer Salmaan does the role of Jomon, son of a business tycoon named Vincent (Mukesh) from Thrissur. Muthumani and Vinu Mohan are said to be playing Jomon's older sister and brother. The story revolves around this family and it also concentrates on the father-son relationship.Source:Wikipedia
