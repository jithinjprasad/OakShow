Plot

Thiruttu Payale 2 is a Tamil movie starring Bobby Simha, Prasanna and Amala Paul in prominent roles. The cast also includes Sanam Shetty, Vivek and Robo Shankar. It is a drama directed by Susi Ganeshan with Vidyasagar as the music composer and Raja Mohammad as the editor forming part of the crew.
