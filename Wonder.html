  • Jacob Tremblay as August "Auggie" Matthew Pullman, a young boy with a facial deformity
  • Izabela Vidovic as Olivia "Via" Pullman, Auggie's older sister
  • Maccie Margaret Chbosky as young Via
  • Mandy Patinkin as Mr. Tushman, the principal of Beecher Prep
  • Daveed Diggs as Mr. Browne, Auggie's English teacher and homeroom teacher
  • Sonia Braga as Lisa "Grans", Isabel's mother, grandmother to Via and Auggie
  • Danielle Rose Russell as Miranda, Via's best friend
  • Nadji Jeter as Justin, Via's boyfriend
  • Noah Jupe as Jack Will, Auggie's best friend
