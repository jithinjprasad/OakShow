Plot

Two imaginative elementary school students, George Beard (Kevin Hart) and Harold Hutchins (Thomas Middleditch), hypnotize their principal, Mr. Krupp (Ed Helms), into becoming a superhero they created known as Captain Underpants. Together, they set out to a journey to stop the mad scientist Professor Poopypants (Nick Kroll), his giant minion the Turbo Toilet 2000, and henchman Melvin Sneedly (Jordan Peele) from destroying Captain Underpants, taking over the world, and annihilating laughter forever.
