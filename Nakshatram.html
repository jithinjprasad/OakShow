Plot

Nakshatram is a Telugu-language action film written and directed by Krishna Vamsi. Jointly produced by K. Srinivasulu, S. Venugopal and Sajju, it features an ensemble cast of Sundeep Kishan, Regina Cassandra, Sai Dharam Tej, Pragya Jaiswal, Prakash Raj, J. D. Chakravarthy, and Tanish. Principal photography commenced in April 2016.
