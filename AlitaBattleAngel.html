Cast with Roles

  • Rosa Salazar as Alita, the titular cyborg heroine. Salazar portrays the role by way of motion capture.
  • Christoph Waltz as Dr. Dyson Ido, a scientist who is Alita's caretaker.
  • Jennifer Connelly as Dr. Chiren,[14] ex-girlfriend of Ido, who escaped with him from the false utopia of Zalem/Tiphares, but became disenchanted with real freedom.
  • Mahershala Ali as Vector, a man who rigs Motorball combat matches.
  • Ed Skrein as Zapan, a cyborg tasked with tracking down and killing Alita.
  • Jackie Earle Haley as Grewishka, a huge cyborg based on Grewcica from the Battle Angel OVA.
  • Keean Johnson as Hugo, Alita's love interest who also teaches her to play a gladiator-style game called Motorball.
  • Michelle Rodriguez as Gelda, a cyborg warrior from Mars who trained Alita.
  • Eiza González as Nyssiana, a cyborg tasked with tracking down and killing Alita.
  • Lana Condor as Koyomi, an orphaned teen and freelance photographer.
  • Jorge Lendeborg Jr. as Tanji, a friend of Hugo's.
  • Idara Victor as Nurse Gerhad, Ido’s aide.
  • Leonard Wu as Kinuba
  • Marko Zaror as Ajakutty, a Motorball player.
  • Elle LaMont as Screwhead, a cyborg tasked with tracking down and killing Alita.
  • Casper Van Dien as Amok

Director: Robert Rodriguez

Producer(s):James Cameron,Jon Landau

Screenplay:James Cameron,Laeta Kalogridis

Based On: Gunnm by Yukito Kishiro
