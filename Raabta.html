Description

Cast: Sushant Singh Rajput, Kriti Sanon, Rajkumar Rao, Varun Sharma, Nidhi Subbaiah, Jim Sarbh, Karan Singh Chhabra, Deepika Padukone

Director: Dinesh Vijan

Narrator: Irrfan Khan

Raabta is an Indian film directed by Dinesh Vijan and produced by Homi Adajania, Bhushan Kumar and Dinesh Vijan. Raabta is a Urdu word which means connection. The film stars Sushant Singh Rajput and Kriti Sanon in lead roles. It is scheduled for release on 9 June 2017. Source: Wikipedia
