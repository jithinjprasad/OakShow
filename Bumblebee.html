Cast: Hailee Steinfeld, John Cena, Jorge Lendeborg Jr., John Ortiz, Jason Drucker, Pamela Adlon

Cast(Humans)

  • Hailee Steinfeld as Charlie Watson, a teenage girl who finds and befriends Bumblebee
  • John Cena as Jack Burns, an agent of Sector 7
  • Jorge Lendeborg Jr as Memo, Charlie's neighbor
  • Jason Drucker Otis Watson, Charlie's younger brother
  • Pamela Adlon as Sally Watson, Charlie and Otis' mother
  • Stephen Schneider as Ron, Charlie's stepfather
  • Ricardo Hoyos as Tripp Summers, Charlie’s classmate
  • John Ortiz as Dr. Powell, an agent of Sector 7
  • Glynn Turman as General Whalen, Burns' superior at Sector 7
  • Len Cariou as Hank, Charlie's uncle and owner of a junkyard where she sources car parts
  • Gracie Dzienny as Tina, Charlie’s mean-spirited classmate
  • Fred Dryer as Sheriff Lock, a sheriff who gets into a high-speed chase with Charlie and Bumblebee
  • Lenny Jacobson as Ray, a trailer park resident killed by the Decepticons
  • Megyn Price as Amber, Ray's girlfriend
  • Nick Pilla as Seymour Simmons, an agent of Sector 7. John Turturro portrayed an older version of the character in previous live-action Transformers films
  • Edwin Hodge as Danny, an agent of Sector 7
  • Tim Martin Gleason as Charlie's father, who died of a heart attack

Cast(Autobots)

  • Dylan O'Brien as the voice of B-127 / Bumblebee a young Autobot scout who transforms into a yellow Willys MB jeep before switching for a rusty yellow 1967 Volkswagen Beetle and eventually a yellow and black 1977 Chevrolet Camaro
  • Peter Cullen as the voice of Optimus Prime the leader of the Autobot resistance who transforms into a scarlet 1977 Freightliner semi truck
  • Grey Griffin as the voice of Arcee a female Autobot in charge of keeping contact with the capital
  • Steve Blum as the voice of Wheeljack an Autobot scientist
  • Andrew Morgado as the voice of Cliffjumper one of Bumblebee's fellow scouts who is killed by Shatter and Dropkick on the Moons of Saturn
  • Kirk Baily as the voice of Brawn an Autobot commando
  • Dennis Singletary as the voice of Ratchet an Autobot medic

Cast(Decepticons)

  • Angela Bassett as the voice of Shatter a Decepticon triple-changer who transforms into a red Plymouth Satellite and a Harrier Jump Jet
  • Justin Theroux as the voice of Dropkick a Decepticon triple-changer who transforms into a blue AMC Javelin and a Bell AH-1 SuperCobra
  • David Sobolov as the voice of Blitzwing a Decepticon who transforms into a red/white McDonnell Douglas F-4 Phantom II and is responsible for damaging Bumblebee's voice box
  • Jon Bailey as the voices of Shockwave, a Decepticon scientist
  • Jon Bailey also voices Soundwave a Decepticon Communications officer who houses Ravage, a minion that transforms into a cassette tape
  • Kirk Baily as the voice of the Seekers, various Decepticon soldiers under Soundwave, Shockwave, and Starscream's command

Director: Rob Marshall

Based On: Transformers by Hasbro

Plot

On the run in the year of 1987, Bumblebee finds refuge in a junkyard in a small Californian beach town. Charlie, on the cusp of turning 18 and trying to find her place in the world, discovers Bumblebee, battle-scarred and broken.IMDB
