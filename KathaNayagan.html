Description

Cast: Vishnu, Catherine Tresa, Soori, Vijay Sethupathi

Director: Muruganandham

Katha Nayagan (English: Lead Hero) is an upcoming Indian Tamil comedy film directed by Muruganandham. Produced by Vishnu, the film also features him in the lead role alongside Catherine Tresa. Featuring music composed by Sean Roldan, the venture began production in September 2016.Source:Wikipedia
