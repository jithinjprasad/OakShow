Plot

Saravanan Irukka Bayamaen is a Tamil movie starring Udhayanidhi Stalin and Regina Cassandra in prominent roles. The movie also stars Srushti Dange and Soori. The romantic action comedy is directed by Enzhil with D. Imman as the music composer, forming part of the crew. Source: Google
