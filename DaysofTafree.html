Description

Cast:Yash Soni, Ansh Bagri, Sanchay Goswami, Nimisha Mehta, Kinjal Rajpriya, Sarabjeet Bindra, Anuradha Mukharjee

Director:Krishnadev Yagnik

Days of Tafree – In Class; Out of Class is an upcoming comedy Bollywood film written and directed by Krishnadev Yagnik. The film is produced by Anand Pandit and Rashmi Sharma. The film revolves around the lives of seven friends showcasing their journey of growing up, highs and lows of their relationship, love and compassion. The film is scheduled to release on September 23, 2016. The film is a remake of Gujarati film Chhello Divas. Source: Wikipedia
