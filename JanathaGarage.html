Description

Cast:Mohanlal,N. T. Rama Rao Jr, Samantha Ruth Prabhu,Nithya Menen,Unni Mukundan & Kajal Aggarwal

Director:Koratala Siva

Janatha Garage is an upcoming 2016 Indian bilingual action film made in Telugu and Malayalam languages. The film is directed by Koratala Siva and produced by Naveen Yerneni, Y. Ravi Shankar, and C. V. Mohan under their banner Mythri Movie Makers. It features Mohanlal, N. T. Rama Rao Jr., Samantha Ruth Prabhu and Nithya Menen in the lead roles. It also features Saikumar, Tamil actress Devayani, and Malayalam actor Unni Mukundan in other important roles. Kajal Aggarwal will be seen with Tarak in a special appearance in a song.
