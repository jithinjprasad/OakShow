Description

Cast: Nani, Nivetha Thomas, Aadhi Pinisetty, Murali Sharma, Tanikella Bharani, Balireddy Pruthviraj

Director: Shiva Nirvana

Ninnu Kori is a Telugu film directed by debutatant Shiva Nirvana and produced by DVV Danayya under DVV Entertainments. Nani and Nivetha Thomas play the lead roles in the movie while Aadhi Pinisetty plays a pivotal role.
