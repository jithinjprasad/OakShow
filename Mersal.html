Description

Cast: Vijay as Maran, S. J. Surya, Samantha Ruth Prabhu, Kajal Aggarwal, Nithya Menon, Sathyaraj, Vadivelu, Rajendran, Sathyan, Misha Ghoshal, Kovai Sarala, Yogi Babu, Hareesh Peradi, Cheenu Mohan, Shanmuga Singaram

Director: Atlee Kumar

The story of Mersal unfurls in three different time periods, in which Vijay plays three different characters, a doctor, a magician and a village leader from Madurai. A magician and a doctor attempt to solve a healthcare threat that is looming large over the country, and in the process, expose the corruption in the Indian medical industry.
