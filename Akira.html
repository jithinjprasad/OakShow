Based On:Mouna Guru By Santha Kumar

Akira is an upcoming Indian action drama film co written, produced and directed by AR Murugadoss. It is the remake of Tamil film Mouna Guru (2011) and features Sonakshi Sinha in lead role. Source: Wikipedia

Plot

A college student single-handedly takes on four corrupt police officers in a bid to prove her innocence.
