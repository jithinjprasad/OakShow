Description

Cast: Sivakarthikeyan, Fahadh Faasil, Nayanthara, Sneha, Prakash Raj, Thambi Ramaiah, Vijay Vasanth, Vinodhini Vaidyanathan, Rohini, RJ Balaji, Sathish, Yogi Babu, Robo Shankar, Charle

Director: Mohan Raja

Velaikkaran (English: Worker) is a Indian Tamil-language action thriller film written and directed by Mohan Raja. Featuring Sivakarthikeyan,Fahadh Faasil,Nayanthara,Prakash Raj and Thambi Ramaiah.
