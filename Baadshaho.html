Plot

Baadshaho is inspired by real-life events during the Emergency and revolves around stolen gold, a thief (Ajay Devgn), an undercover cop (Vidyut Jammwal), a character inspired by Maharani Gayatri Devi (Ileana D'Cruz) and an army officer (Emraan Hashmi) who is entrusted with protecting the country's treasure.IMDB
