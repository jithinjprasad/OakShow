Plot

When his upcoming marriage to his girlfriend gets cancelled, Ramesh feels down in the dumps and drowns his sorrows in alcohol. In his intoxicated state, the IT expert agrees to his friend's suggestion to forget about his ex by spending a night with a call girl(Anaika Soti). Things take a turn when the call girl arrives at his house while he is away and she ends up being murdered. Ramesh, now a suspect, has to search for the real killer and clear his name.Source:IMDB
