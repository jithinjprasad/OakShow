Description

Cast: Anil Kapoor, Arjun Kapoor as Karanveer Singh and Charanveer Singh, Ileana D'Cruz as Sweety, Karan's girlfriend and Charan's wife, Athiya Shetty as Binkle, Charan's childhood friend who has crush on him and Karan's fiancee., Neha Sharma, Rahul Dev, Karan Kundra, Ratna Pathak, Anil Mange, Pavan Malhotra, Krishan Tandon

Director: Anees Bazmee

Plot

Karan and Charan are identical twin brothers but their personalities are polar opposite. Karan has grown up in London while Charan in Punjab. Karan is street-smart, calculative and flamboyant while Charan is simple, idealistic and honest in his approach. Karan is in love with Sweety while Charan is in a relationship with Nafisa. Karans family fixes Karans wedding with Binkle, daughter of one of the wealthiest families in London. He convinces his family to fix the match with Charan instead. The respective families are happy with this development except Charan because he wants to marry Nafisa.Source: IMDB
