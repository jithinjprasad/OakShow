Description

Cast: Krishna, Anandhi, Nithin Sathya, Karunas, Saravanan, Pandi, Madhusudhan Rao, Aathma Patrick

Director: Feroz

Pandigai is a Tamil movie starring Krishna in a prominent role. The cast also includes Anandhi, Nithin Sathya, Karunas, Saravanan and Madhusudhan Rao. It is an action-thriller movie directed by Feroz.
