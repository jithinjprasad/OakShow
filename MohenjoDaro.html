Description

Cast: Hrithik Roshan,Pooja Hegde,Kabir Bedi,Arunoday Singh,Suhasini Mulay,Nitish Bharadwaj,Kishori Shahane,Sharad Kelkar,Manish Choudhary,Narendra Jha,Piyush Mishra & Casey Frank.

Director: Ashutosh Gowariker

The film is set in 2016 BC in the ancient city of Mohenjo-daro in the era of the Indus Valley civilisation. The film is about a man who falls in love with his enemy's daughter who is a dancer. Centred on romance through the cultures and ancient civilisations of Mohenjo-daro, Gowariker took over three years to develop the film with the help of archaeologists who documented the discovery of Indus Valley civilisation. The film was shot in Bhuj and Mumbai with short schedules in Jabalpur and Thane. Source: Wikipedia
