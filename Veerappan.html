Veerappan is a Indian movie which's based on the lifestory of Indian bandit Veerappan, and the events leading to Operation Cocoon which's meant to capture or kill Veerappan.

Note:This is not a remake of Ram Gopal Varma's Kanada movie Killing Veerappan.
