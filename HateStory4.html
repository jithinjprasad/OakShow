Plot

The girl who will stop at nothing to become a star model in the most coveted advertising agency in London, Urvashi, is ambitious, beautiful and without a doubt voluptuous. Rajveer, the man known to stop at nothing to get the woman he wants spots her talent and pitches her to get the project. Aryan, on the other hand, the man who is smitten by Urvashi at first sight, will go beyond bonds and boundaries, even beyond his own brother Rajveer, to possess who he lusts for in cold blood.

Nasty tricks, crazy ways, bitter hearts, steamy twists and driven by passion, the plot comes to a crescendo amongst media glare. Beyond glamour, broken hearts and red roses lies a bare it all bitter truth that only Urvashi knows. It is a story of greed, passion, and murder in the backdrop of political upheaval and elections in London.Source:BookMyShow
