Cast with Roles

  • Keanu Reeves as John Wick, a legendary hitman on the run.
  • Halle Berry as Sofia, an assassin and a close friend of John Wick.
  • Ruby Rose as Ares, an assassin.
  • Mark Dacascos as Zero, the main assassin with a vendetta.
  • Ian McShane as Winston, the owner and manager of the Continental Hotel in New York.
  • Lance Reddick as Charon, the concierge at the Continental Hotel in New York.
  • Laurence Fishburne as The Bowery King, an underground crime lord.
  • Asia Kate Dillon as The Adjudicator, a member of the High Table.
  • Anjelica Huston as The Director, a member of the High Table and protector of John Wick on the High Table.
  • Robin Lord Taylor as The Administrator, a member of the High Table.
  • Jason Mantzoukas as Tick Tock Man, an assassin
  • Arjon Bashiri as an Assassin
  • John Leguizamo as Aurelio, John Wick’s former best friend and chop shop owner.
  • Common as Cassian

Director: Chad Stahelski

Plot

John Wick is still on the run following his assassination of a member of the High Table in The Continental, and with a $14 million global contract out on himself. Banned from every Continental service, John’s objective is to fight his way out of New York City.Wikipedia
