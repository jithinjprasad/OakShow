Plot

Spark (Jace Norman) is a teenage monkey, and with his friends Vix the fox (Jessica Biel) and Chunk the warthog (Rob deLeeuw), they embark on a mission to take back the Planet Bana from the evil General Zhong (Alan C. Peterson). Spark's quest leads him all over the universe, and to discover the secret of his true identity. Source: Wikipedia
