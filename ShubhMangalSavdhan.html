Director: R.S. Prasanna

Plot

The story is about a boy Mudit Sharma, (Ayushmann Khurrana) who gets engaged to a girl, Sugandha (Bhumi Pednekar). It is going to be an arranged marriage and the extended families of both are looking forward to it. However due to erectile dysfunction, Mudit can't get it up and on the advice of his friends tries out various quacks treating impotency. It is a remake of the tamil movie Kalyana Samayal Sadham starring Prasanna and Lekha Washington.Wikipedia
