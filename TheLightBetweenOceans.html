Synopsis

Tom Sherbourne, a lighthouse keeper and war veteran, and his wife Isabel are living off the coast in post World War I Western Australia. One day, the couple rescue a baby girl who has washed up on an adrift rowboat and decide to informally adopt her as their own, whom they name Lucy. But as Lucy grows older, Tom and Isabel discover the consequences of raising the child when a visit to the mainland and an encounter with a particular woman, threaten to break apart their blissfully happy family.
