Premise

A new student named Rafe Khatchadorian (Griffin Gluck) can't wait to start his first day at Hills Village Middle School, but it's not as glamorous as he believes when Principal Dwight (Andy Daly) and most teachers who are in charge of him, are cruel and worse than the bullies. So after Rafe & his friends have finally had enough of them, they come up with an idea to start an insurrection to go up against their out-of-line principal where they do things that would humiliate him.
