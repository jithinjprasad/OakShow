Plot summary

Two 20-something-year-old arms dealers, Efraim Diveroli and David Packouz, receive government contracts to supply weapons for U.S. troops in Afghanistan. David Packouz is a weed smoking massage therapist whose life changes when he finds out that his girlfriend is pregnant. He meets with his long time friend Efraim Diveroli who asks him to join him on his business. He owns AEY which is the company that supplies the U.S. military with weapons and ammunition. They win bigger and bigger contracts until they are awarded a $300 million dollar contract to arm the Afghan Army which is their ultimate downfall.Source: Wikipedia
