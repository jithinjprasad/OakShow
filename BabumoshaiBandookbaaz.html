Description

Cast: Nawazuddin Siddiqui, Bidita Bag, Jatin Goswami, Shraddha Das, Anil George, Bhagwan Tiwari, Aryan Ashik, Jeetu Shivhare, Murli Sharma, Divya Dutta, Sachin Chaubey

Director: Kushan Nandy

Plot

The movie, filled with humor and romance, is a quirky ride into the life and times of a small time contract killer, Babu, played by Nawazuddin Siddiqui. The film spins around his love, his friends, his rivalries and his revenge.SourceIMDB
