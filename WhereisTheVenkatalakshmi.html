    Plot

    The film is set in a remote village and revolves around the story of four characters: Venkatalakshmi, Chanti, Pandu and Veera Reddy. With the arrival of Venkatalakshmi as the new school teacher in the village, a series of interesting events involving these four unfold, leading up to unexpected consequences.Source:BookMyShow
