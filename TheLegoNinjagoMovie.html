Based On: Lego Ninjago by The Lego Group

Plot

Six teenagers living in the far-away land of Ninjago find themselves "hired" and trained by an old ninja master named Wu, to become ninjas who protect the capital city at night from monsters sent by Lord Garmadon, a warlord seeking to conquer Ninjago, while using a variety of vehicles and dragons to aid them. Meanwhile, Lloyd Garmadon, the group's Green Ninja and leader, begins asking his mother questions about his father, and soon finds himself struggling to accept the truth that Lord Garmadon is his father, as a new threat soon emerges to endanger Ninjago.Source: Wikipedia
