Plot

An unlikely love triangle unfolds when married professor, Shyam, has an illicit affair with his student, Sandhya, who in turn is trying to be wooed by her classmate Kamal. Set in a small town in Madhya Pradesh, Haraamkhor is a prohibited love story witnessed through the eyes of two adolescents..Source:BookMyShow
