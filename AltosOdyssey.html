Description

Just beyond the horizon sits a majestic desert, vast and unexplored.Join Alto and his friends and set off on an endless sandboarding journey to discover its secrets.Soar above windswept dunes, traverse thrilling canyons, and explore long-hidden temples in a fantastical place far from home.Along the way, you’ll grind across vines, bounce atop hot air balloons, ride towering rock walls, and escape mischievous lemurs – all while uncovering the desert’s many mysteries.
