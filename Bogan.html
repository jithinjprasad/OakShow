Description

Cast: Jayam Ravi, Arvind Swamy, Akshara Gowda, Hansika Motwani, Nassar, Nagendra Prasad, VTV Ganesh, Aadukalam Naren, Raju Sundaram, Prabhu Deva

Director: Lakshman

Producer: Prabhu Deva

Bogan is a Indian Tamil action film written and directed by Lakshman and produced by Prabhu Deva. The film stars Jayam Ravi, Arvind Swamy, Hansika Motwani and Akshara Gowda in the leading roles. Bogan is inspired from the tamil movie Gulebakavali.
