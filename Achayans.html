Plot

The movie Achayans is about 5 heroes and 3 heroines. The Achayans belonging to the thottathil family set out on a journey to find extreme fun and enjoyment. Accidentally, two girls join their journey. The rest of the journey unfolds with lots of twists and turns. Be ready to be a part of this ride full of extravagant fun and thrill.Source: BookMyShow
