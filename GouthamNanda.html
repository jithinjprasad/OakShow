Plot

Goutham Nanda is an Telugu movie starring Tottempudi Gopichand and Hansika Motwani in prominent roles. The movie also stars Catherine Tresa. It is a romantic action drama directed by Sampath Nandi with S. Thaman as musician, forming part of the crew. SourceBookMyShow
