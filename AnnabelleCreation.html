Plot

Samuel Mullins, a dollmaker, and his wife Esther, whose daughter died twelve years earlier, open their home to Sister Charlotte, a nun, and several girls from an orphanage that has been closed. The dollmaker's possessed creation, Annabelle, sets her sights on the girls, turning their shelter into a storm of terror.Source:Wikipedia
