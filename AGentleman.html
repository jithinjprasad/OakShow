Director: Raj & D. K (Raj Nidimoru and Krishna D.K.)

Plot

Gaurav attempts to woo Kavya, pursuing her into marrying him, however, she is not prepared to take the plunge. She wishes to find a someone who is a gentleman, takes risks in life and lives his life to the fullest. However, their lives take an interesting turn after one of Gaurav's assignments takes him to Mumbai.SourceBookMyShow
