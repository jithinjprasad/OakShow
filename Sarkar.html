Description

Director(s): A. R. Murugadoss

Produced by: Kalanithi Maran

Plot

Based on interviews with K Bhagyaraj of Film Writers' Association, the plot of the movie revolves around a successful NRI CEO who returns to India for voting in elections but finds his vote has already been exercised. He fights back and the elections are cancelled. An enmity develops with politicians and he joins politics to compete in elections and whether he succeeded in his fight against establishment forms the rest of the story. Director A.R.Murugadoss has mentioned that he was inspired by a real life event in Thespian actor Sivaji Ganesan.Source: Wikipedia
