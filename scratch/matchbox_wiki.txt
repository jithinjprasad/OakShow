'''''Matchbox: The Movie''''' is a 2026 American [[action comedy film]] directed by [[Sam Hargrave]] and written by David Coggeshall, based on the toy brand [[Matchbox (brand)|Matchbox]] by [[Mattel]]. It stars [[John Cena]], [[Jessica Biel]], [[Sam Richardson (actor)|Sam Richardson]], [[Arturo Castro (Guatemalan actor)|Arturo Castro]], and [[Teyonah Parris]].

''Matchbox: The Movie'' was released in the United States on [[Apple TV (streaming service)|Apple TV]] on October 9, 2026. It received mixed-to-negative reviews from critics.

==Plot==
Spy and former soldier Sean Walker reunites with childhood friends only to be drugged and kidnapped by a group looking for a [[Nuclear weapon|nuclear warhead]]. The [[CIA]] believes Walker and his friends stole the nuclear weapon, so the group decides to re-steal the bomb and clear their names.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |last=Ouellette |first=Jennifer |date=2026-07-26 |title=First teaser for Apple TV's Neuromancer debuts at SDCC |url=https://arstechnica.com/culture/2026/07/first-teaser-for-apple-tvs-neuromancer-debuts-at-sdcc/ |access-date=2026-07-29 |website=Ars Technica |language=en}}</ref>
