Plot Summery

Jai Singh Rathod is back. But he isn't the same person anymore, at least on the surface. He's battling alcohol issues in rehab. That's not it. His son holds him responsible for his mother's death, and hates him. Jai gets a terror mastermind Roshan Sherchan arrested along with accomplice Shivani Malik. But Roshan's brother Haroon Sherchan has made a deadly plan. He threatens to infect Mumbai with a virus that can put everyone's life at risk. While the Anti Terrorism Squad is trying its best to solve the case, Jai Rathod is hatching a plan to free Roshan. What's his motive? There's a new woman in Jai's life, and his son starts hating him even more for this. Jai apparently is doing everything for the sake of Surveen, who's also seen romancing Haroon aka Sikander Kher. What's her truth?.Source:Wikipedia
