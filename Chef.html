Plot

The Hindi adaptation of Jon Favreau's 2014 Hollywood movie by the same name, Chef is a unique tale of Roshan Kalra's journey to find out his true priorities and source of happiness. It's a story of food, love, family, togetherness, and that of a father's rediscovery of the bond with his son. Source: BookMyShow
