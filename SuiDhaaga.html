Director: Sharat Katariya

Plot

Sui Dhaaga - Made in India recounts the journey of an honest man, who rises from his humble beginnings to become a celebrated social entrepreneur. A heartwarming story of pride, perseverance and self-reliance, the film talks about the need of social entrepreneurship as a tool for the social and economic development of our artisans, who are living their life on the margins due to the changing times.Source:BookMyShow
