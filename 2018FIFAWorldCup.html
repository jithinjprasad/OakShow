Description

The 2018 FIFA World Cup will be the 21st FIFA World Cup, a quadrennial international football tournament contested by the men's national teams of the member associations of FIFA.

Venues: Kaliningrad, Kazan, Krasnodar, Moscow, Nizhny Novgorod, Rostov-on-Don, Saint Petersburg, Samara, Saransk, Sochi, Volgograd, Yaroslavl, and Yekaterinburg.

Total number of Stadiums: 12

  1. Samara: Samara Arena (seating capacity during the FIFA World Cup: 45,000)
  2. Nizhny Novgorod: Nizhny Novgorod Stadium (seating capacity during the FIFA World Cup: 45,000)
  3. Volgograd: Volgograd Arena (seating capacity during the FIFA World Cup: 45,000)
  4. Ekaterinburg: Ekaterinburg Arena (seating capacity during the FIFA World Cup: 35,000)
  5. Saransk: Mordovia Arena (seating capacity during the FIFA World Cup: 44,000)
  6. Rostov-on-Don: Rostov Arena (seating capacity during the FIFA World Cup: 45,000)
  7. Kaliningrad: Kaliningrad Stadium (seating capacity during the FIFA World Cup: 35,000)
  8. Kazan: Kazan Arena (seating capacity during the FIFA World Cup: 45,000)
  9. Moscow: Spartak Stadium (seating capacity during the FIFA World Cup: 45,000)
  10. Sochi: Fisht Stadium (seating capacity during the FIFA World Cup: 45,000)
  11. Saint Petersburg: Saint Petersburg Stadium (seating capacity during the FIFA World Cup: 67,000)
  12. Moscow: Luzhniki Stadium (seating capacity during the FIFA World Cup: 80,000)
