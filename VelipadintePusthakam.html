Description

Cast: Mohanlal, Anna Rajan, Salim Kumar, Siddique, Arun Kurian, Sarath Kumar, Jude Anthany Joseph, Priyanka Nair, Anoop Menon, Krishna Kumar, Kalabhavan Shajohn, Shivaji Guruvayoor, Alencier Ley Lopez, Rishan Nambyattil

Director: Lal Jose

Velipadinte Pusthakam (English: The Book of Revelation) is an upcoming Indian Malayalam comedy film directed by Lal Jose and written by Benny P. Nayarambalam. The film produced by Aashirvad Cinemas stars Mohanlal in the lead role, with Anna Rajan, Anoop Menon, Arun Kurian, Sarath Kumar, Priyanka Nair, Siddique, Salim Kumar, Alencier Ley Lopez, and Kalabhavan Shajohn in supporting roles. Shaan Rahman composed the film's soundtrack and score.Source: Wikipedia
