Description

Cast: Andrea Jeremiah,Vasanth Ravi,Anjali & Azhagam Perumal

Director: Ram

Taramani is a 2017 Indian Tamil-language drama film written and directed by Ram. He co-produced the film with J. Satish Kumar under his production company Catamaran Productions. The film features Andrea Jeremiah and Vasanth Ravi. The venture began production in August 2013. Anjali is appearing in a special role.
