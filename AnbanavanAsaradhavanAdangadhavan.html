Theme

Anbanavan Asaradhavan Adangadhavan (English: Lovable Man, Uninfluenced Man, Uncontrollable Man) also known as AAA, is an upcoming Indian two-part Tamil-language action comedy film written and directed by Adhik Ravichandran and produced by S. Michael Rayappan under banner of Global Infotainment. The film features Silambarasan, Shriya Saran, Tamannaah Bhatia and Sana Khan in the lead roles, while Simbu plays his first triple role and Mahat Raghavendra, Kovai Sarala, VTV Ganesh in a supporting roles.The film has music composed by Yuvan Shankar Raja, cinematography handled by Krishnan Vasant and editing done by Ruben.Source:Wikipedia
