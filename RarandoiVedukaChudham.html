Plot

Rarandoi Veduka Chudham is a Telugu movie starring Naga Chaitanya and Rakul Preet Singh in prominent roles. The cast also includes Jagapathi Babu and Sampath Raj. It is a drama directed by Kalyan Krishna with Devi Sri Prasad as the Music composer forming part of the crew. Source:BookMyShow
