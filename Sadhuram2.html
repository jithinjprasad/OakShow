Description

Cast:Yog Japee,Sanam Shetty,Rohit Nair & Riaz Ansari

Director: Sumanth Radhakrishnan

Sadhuram 2 is India's first philanthropic thriller and the first ever movie in India where the second part is getting released before the first part. It is also a crowd-funded film made from the contribution of fans of James Wan`s Saw franchise.
