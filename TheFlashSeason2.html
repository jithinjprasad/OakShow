The Flash Season 2 Full Episode List

Episode 45 Overview

Zoom's metahuman army wreaks havoc across the city. Barry puts out several fires and takes out some of the metahumans to help the police. Back at S.T.A.R. Labs, Barry displays a level of confidence in stopping Zoom that concerns the team, and each of them attempts to convince him to be more cautious. Caitlin returns to the team, but not without emotional trauma from being kidnapped. A metahuman begins attacking various buildings around Central City using a sonic blast. Barry confronts the metahuman, who turns out to be Laurel Lance's doppelganger, "Black Siren". She initially gets the upper hand, but Barry is rescued by Wally, who is trying to help out in order to repay the Flash. The team creates a sonic amplifier tuned to the frequency of Earth-2 natives. They discharge it and render all Earth-2 metahumans unconscious, allowing Barry to apprehend them all at once, although Zoom escapes. Later, Zoom returns and kidnaps Henry in front of the team. Barry races after them, revealing his identity to Wally, and is forced to watch Zoom kill his father. Source: Wikepedia

Episode 46 Overview

Zoom comes to Barry and demands a race to determine who is faster, and states he will kill everyone Barry loves if he refuses. Wells realizes that Zoom stole a magnatar from Mercury Labs that will allow him to destroy every world in the multiverse if he and Barry can create enough energy from their race to power the device. When Barry refuses to back out of the race, the team decides to lock him in a containment cell for his own protection. The team develops their own plan, which sends Zoom back to Earth-2, but Joe is pulled in with him. In Earth-2, Zoom reveals that the man in the iron mask is the real Jay Garrick. Wally releases Barry to help bring back Joe. Barry agrees to race Zoom if he returns Joe unharmed. As Barry and Zoom race, the magnatar begins to charge. Barry takes out Zoom, and creates a time remnant of himself to stop the magnatar. Defeated, Barry allows the time wraiths to take Zoom. The real Jay is revealed to be Henry's doppelganger. Wells and Jesse return to Earth-2 so they can help Jay return to Earth-3. Unable to handle his father's death, Barry travels back in time and stops Reverse-Flash from killing his mother. Source: Wikepedia
