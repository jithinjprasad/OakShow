Summary:

A duplicitous, aashram based, Indian Godman's good deeds serve activities criminal and unholy, such as rapes, murders, drugs, vote bank politics and forced male emasculation. The law and a few crusaders investigate to bring him to account.SourceIMDB

Plot

The story revolves around a Baba (preacher), played by Bobby Deol, known as 'Baba Nirala.' Whose followers have blind faith in him and will do anything that he asks of them. But it is for sure that this Baba Nirala is a conman, who makes sure that his devotees leave behind their wealth to him and stay attached to the Aashram for life.

Meanwhile, Darshan Kumaar is a cop in this seeirs and is adamant about unraveling the truth behind the skeletons and dead bodies recovered from the Asharam's property.

Every episode unfolds a mystery that revolves around the Baba Nirala. Whether the cop will find evidence and prove anything is left for the Part II of 'Aashram' season 1.All episodes of Season 2 airs on 11th November 2020.SourceWikipedia

Aashram Season 1 All Episodes


Epsiode Title Poster
S1-Ep1 Pran Pratishtha

Pammi has always faced discrimination and tried her best to raise her voice against the system. But then, like a ray of hope, Baba Nirala comes to her. And then, a body is dug out from a construction site in a forest.

S1-Ep2 Grih Pravesh

The whole town is in rage when the skeleton is discovered in the forest and Sunderlal's name comes up in the case. Ujagar Singh is assigned to the case. Pammi, on the other hand, is drawn to the magic of Baba Nirala's devotion.

S1-Ep3 Duh Swapna

Pammi has decided to give up her career as a wrestler and follow the path of devotion and salvation under the guidance of Baba Nirala. Meanwhile Ujagar Singh is probing the case of the skeleton.

S1-Ep4 Sewa Daar

Baba has allowed Pammi to join the ashram as a sadhvi. While investigating the case of the skeleton, Ujagar Singh gets pushed towards the ashram and suspects some illegal activities going on there.

S1-Ep5 Amrit Sudha
Baba Nirala has organized the event of Mass Marriage in the ashram, where Satti would be one of the grooms. Hukum Singh offers Baba a luring deal. Ujagar Singh makes progress on the case when the skeleton gets identified by a girl.

S1-Ep6 Vish Haran

Baba comes to know about I.G. Sharma's investigation. Sub Inspector Ujagar Singh in his investigation has led him to a lead inside the ashram named Kavita.

S1-Ep7 Gati Rodh

Ujagar Singh has dug too deep into the secrets of the ashram. Bhopa, on the other hand receives shocking information about someone they had been following.

S1-Ep8 Shuddhi Karan

Baba Nirala wants more support from the younger generation Tinka Singh refuses for the show. Meanwhile, Babita's looks have created an impression on Baba and he tries to remove Satti from his way.

S1-Ep9 Maha Prasad

All the efforts of Ujagar Singh to continue the investigation have failed. Inside the ashram, Satti has decided to do Shuddhikaran. Babita is shattered on learning this. She is called by Baba to his private quarters.
