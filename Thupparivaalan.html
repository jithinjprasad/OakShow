Plot

Thupparivaalan (English: Detective Agent) is a Indian Tamil detective fantasy thriller film written and directed by Mysskin. The film is produced by Vishal, who also stars in the lead role.A talented ensemble cast follows starring: Prasanna, Vinay Rai, Simran, Andrea Jeremiah, Anu Emmanuel, John Vijay and K. Bhagyaraj. The project was launched in March 2016, and has completed shooting and is in post production. The planned release date is September 14th, 2017 Wikipedia
